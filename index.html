Air DrawAR

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Air DrawAR does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything the app does happens on your device. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, third-party SDKs or servers.

Camera

The app uses the camera to show your surroundings in augmented reality and to detect your index finger and pinch gesture so you can draw. Camera frames are processed on your device by Apple's ARKit and Vision frameworks and are never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Face Data (TrueDepth API)

Air DrawAR does not collect, store, share or transmit face data.

When you switch to the front camera, the app starts an ARKit face-tracking session, which uses the TrueDepth camera. The app uses this for one purpose only: to keep the augmented reality view stable and correctly positioned while the front camera is active, so your drawing stays anchored in the room as you move.

The app does not request, read or process the face mesh, facial expression (blend shape) values, or any face depth information that ARKit can provide, and it does not identify or recognize anyone. Nothing derived from the TrueDepth camera is saved to your device, included in saved scenes, sent off your device, or shared with any third party. This data exists only in memory while the front camera is in use, and is discarded as soon as you switch cameras, leave the drawing screen, or close the app.

If you tap the shutter button, the app saves a picture of what is on screen, which may include your face when the front camera is in use, to your own photo library. That happens only when you ask for it, the image goes only to your photo library, and it is never sent to the developer.

Photos

When you tap the shutter button, the app asks for permission to add that snapshot to your photo library. It uses add-only access and cannot see or read your existing photos.

Data Stored on Your Device

Scenes you save are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are included in your device backups if you use them, and are removed when you delete the app.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any information, because the app does not collect any.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your Choices

  • Turn camera or photo access on or off at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Delete saved scenes from the Scenes menu in the app.
  • Delete snapshots from the Photos app.
  • Delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
