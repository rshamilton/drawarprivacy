DrawAR

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 10, 2026

DrawAR is an augmented reality drawing app. The current version of the app processes camera, motion, and hand-tracking input on-device and does not send that data to the developer's servers.

Information the App Uses

  • Camera access is used to render the AR view and detect hand position for drawing.
  • Motion data may be used by iOS AR frameworks to improve tracking stability.
  • Photo library access is requested only when you choose to save a snapshot.

Data Collection

DrawAR does not require account creation and does not collect personal information on the developer's servers in the current shipped build.

On-Device Storage

The app stores saved scenes locally on your device so you can reopen your drawings later. If you save a snapshot, the image is written to your Photos library through iOS.

Tracking And Advertising

DrawAR does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Your Choices

  • You can deny permissions in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete saved scenes inside the app.
  • You can remove saved snapshots from the Photos app.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove local app data, subject to device backups you control.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at draw-ar@googlegroups.com.
